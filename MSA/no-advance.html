WHEREAS the Contractor wishes to be engaged by the Client for telecommunication tower and associated civil, mechanical, and electrical works, on the terms of the Client’s Master Service Agreement and the purchase orders or statements of work issued under it.

NOW THE CONTRACTOR DECLARES as follows:

1. No advance payment

The Contractor shall not request, demand, or accept any advance payment, deposit, down payment, or mobilisation payment from the Client before the works, or the relevant milestone, described in the applicable purchase order or statement of work have been performed. The Contractor is not entitled to any such payment.

2. Capacity to mobilise

The Contractor has, and will maintain, sufficient working capital, bank facilities, or supplier credit to mobilise, carry out, and complete each purchase order or statement of work up to the agreed milestone before submitting a claim for payment.

3. Payment only as agreed

The Contractor shall claim payment only for milestones achieved, and only in accordance with Section 4 of the Master Service Agreement and the payment terms of the applicable purchase order or statement of work. A claim shall be supported by the documents that purchase order or statement of work requires.

4. No variation by later request

A request for advance payment, whether oral or written, does not vary this Declaration or the Master Service Agreement. The Client may reject it without affecting the Contractor’s obligation to proceed with the works.

5. Continuing effect

This Declaration applies to every purchase order or statement of work accepted while it remains in force, and it continues until the Client agrees otherwise in writing.
